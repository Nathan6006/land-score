# Species table sources and status

**Status: draft, needs review by a forester or Roots lead before use (issue: species lookup).**

Columns:
- `drainage_min` / `drainage_max`: soil survey drainage classes the species tolerates.
  1 excessively, 2 somewhat excessively, 3 well, 4 moderately well, 5 somewhat poorly,
  6 poorly, 7 very poorly drained.
- `ph_min` / `ph_max`: soil pH range.
- `shade_tolerance`, `deer_browse`: rough categories for planning, not measurements.

Compiled from general silvics references: USDA Forest Service *Silvics of North America*
(Agriculture Handbook 654), USDA NRCS PLANTS database fact sheets, and Ohio DNR Division of
Forestry native tree guidance. Ranges were simplified to whole drainage classes and should be
checked against those sources species by species. Ash (Fraxinus) and American elm are left out
because of emerald ash borer and Dutch elm disease.
